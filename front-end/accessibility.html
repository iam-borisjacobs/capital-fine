Commitment to Accessibility & Barrier-Free Banking

Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) & Accessible Canada Act

1. Web & Digital Standards

Royal Bank digital properties are developed in alignment with the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, including keyboard navigation, high contrast text ratios, and screen reader semantic labeling.

2. Assistive In-Branch Services

All RBC branches feature wheelchair ramps, automatic entrance doors, audio-enabled ATMs with tactile Braille keypads and high-contrast screens, and support for certified service animals.

3. Alternate Formats & Teletype (TTY)

Monthly bank statements and financial literature are available upon request in Braille, large print, and accessible digital PDF formats. TTY telephone support is available toll-free at 1-800-661-1275.
